# Three Pricing Guide Versions - Overview

**All three use the same pricing structure:**
- Base credit value: $4,000
- Starter Package: 5 credits = $20,000
- Quarterly Package: 20 credits = $72,000 (10% discount)
- Annual Package: 80 credits = $288,000 (10% discount)

---

## Version 1: SIMPLE (`PRICING_GUIDE_V1_SIMPLE.md`)

### **What It Is**
Straightforward, comprehensive, traditional pricing guide

### **Structure**
1. Bridges from the two workshops they already know
2. Introduces three-level cascade concept
3. Explains credit system clearly
4. Shows credit costs for all services
5. Presents three packages with examples
6. Shows five deployment scenarios
7. Addresses value multiplication
8. FAQ section
9. Complete 20-item menu at bottom

### **Tone**
Professional, clear, informative

### **Best For**
- Traditional fund company buyers
- Finance/procurement teams
- People who want all details upfront
- First presentation to management team

### **Strengths**
- Most comprehensive
- Easiest to scan for specific information
- All questions answered in one document
- Traditional format feels familiar

### **Weaknesses**
- Longest (could feel overwhelming)
- Less narrative/story-driven
- More "vendor guide" than "strategic partner"

---

## Version 2: FRAMEWORK (`PRICING_GUIDE_V2_FRAMEWORK.md`)

### **What It Is**
Framework-driven guide inspired by your newsletter style

### **Structure**
1. Opens with "Strategic Snapshot" story (like newsletter)
2. Introduces "Strategic AI Enablement Lens" (4 dimensions: Depth/Reach/Levels/Commitment)
3. Shows five deployment "patterns" through the lens
4. Credit system explained through the framework
5. Packages positioned as commitment dimension
6. Value multiplication shown as "what the lens reveals"
7. FAQ + complete menu

### **Tone**
Consultative, insight-driven, strategic partner

### **Best For**
- Strategic buyers (VP-level, C-suite)
- Innovation-minded fund companies
- Long sales cycle where you're educating them
- When you want to differentiate from typical vendors

### **Strengths**
- Most aligned with your newsletter voice
- Positions you as strategic thinker, not vendor
- Gives them a framework they can use beyond pricing
- Creates "aha moments" (the lens reveals what they couldn't see)
- Reinforces your brand: frameworks unlock possibilities

### **Weaknesses**
- Requires more cognitive engagement
- Some buyers may just want "what does it cost?"
- Framework concept may not land with tactical buyers

---

## Version 3: VISUAL (`PRICING_GUIDE_V3_VISUAL.md`)

### **What It Is**
One-page, scannable, table-heavy visual guide

### **Structure**
1. Quick bridge from two workshops
2. Visual three-level cascade diagram (ASCII)
3. How credits work (table format)
4. Three packages (condensed)
5. Five deployment examples (table)
6. Value multiplication (bullet points)
7. Quick FAQ
8. Complete menu (all tables)

### **Tone**
Efficient, visual, get-to-the-point

### **Best For**
- Busy executives who skim
- Second/third touchpoint (after they've seen detailed version)
- Leave-behind after presentation
- Internal sharing (easy to forward)
- Printing as one-pager

### **Strengths**
- Fastest to scan
- Easiest to print/share
- Tables make comparisons easy
- No fluff, just information
- Works great as reference document

### **Weaknesses**
- Less persuasive (assumes they're already interested)
- No story/narrative
- May need verbal explanation to land fully
- Less differentiation (could be anyone's pricing guide)

## How to Use Each Version

### **Scenario 1: Cold Outreach or First Meeting**
**Use:** Version 2 (Framework)
**Why:** Establishes you as strategic thinker, creates curiosity, differentiates from typical vendors
**Follow-up:** Send Version 3 as PDF leave-behind

### **Scenario 2: RFP or Procurement Process**
**Use:** Version 1 (Simple)
**Why:** Answers all questions comprehensively, traditional format, easy to compare to competitors
**Support with:** Version 3 as executive summary

### **Scenario 3: Presenting to Management Team**
**Use:** Version 2 for presentation, Version 3 as handout
**Why:** Framework version tells the story verbally, visual version is easy reference
**Follow-up:** Send Version 1 for detailed review

### **Scenario 4: Quick Follow-Up / Internal Sharing**
**Use:** Version 3 (Visual)
**Why:** Fast to scan, easy to forward, no narrative needed
**Context:** "Here's the one-pager we discussed"

### **Scenario 5: Strategic Partnership Discussion**
**Use:** Version 2 (Framework)
**Why:** Positions conversation at strategic level, invites collaborative design
**Outcome:** "Let's use the lens to design your deployment strategy"
