4. Dashboard Section 📊

The Progress Command Center

This is where users spend most of their time - seeing their Mission, Journeys, Components, and active Quests.

Mission Overview

  • Active Mission status
  • Activated Journeys (4-6 typically)
  • Overall progress toward Mission
  • Time invested this month

Journey Grid/List/Timeline

  • All 12 Journeys with maturity badges
  • Component counts & levels per Journey
  • Active Quest indicators
  • Priority rankings (user-set)

Active Quests Widget

  • In-progress Quests (cross-Journey)
  • Completion % per Quest
  • "Continue" quick actions
  • Next recommended Quest

Story Trail Preview

  • Recent completions
  • Level-up celebrations
  • This week's achievements
  • Link to full Story Trail

Note: This is covered extensively in the Visual Experience Design document - see Dashboard View Options section.

8. Analytics Section 📈

Business Intelligence & Measurement

This is where the Outcome tracking happens - measuring actual business results from Component advancement.

Component-Level Metrics

  • Current maturity level (L1-L5)
  • Progress toward next level
  • Time at current level
  • Advancement velocity
  • Quest completion rate
  • Deliverable quality scores

Business Outcome Tracking

  • Revenue impact ($ per Component)
  • Time savings (hours per Component)
  • Client satisfaction changes
  • Lead generation increases
  • Conversion rate improvements
  • Efficiency gains (% improvement)

Journey-Level Analytics

  • Overall Journey maturity
  • Component coverage (%)
  • Quest completion count
  • Time investment (hours)
  • Journey priority vs progress
  • Recommended next actions

Mission Progress

  • Journey constellation health
  • Overall business transformation
  • Time to achievement tracking
  • ROI calculation (investment vs results)
  • Peer comparison (anonymized)
  • Progress reports (weekly/monthly)

Note: See Visual Experience Design document Section 6 (Outcome Tracking View) for detailed mockups.

Mirror/Map/Machine Sessions

  • 1-on-1 facilitated sessions with Elizabeth
  • Pre-work Sparks required
  • AI synthesis before session
  • Post-session deliverable generation
  • Follow 48-hour execution pattern

Workshops (Credit-Based)

  • Regional cohorts (10-30 advisors)
  • LinkedIn Positioning (2 credits)
  • Tech Stack Optimization (2 credits)
  • Custom GPTs provided
  • Peer networking included

Office Hours

  • Drop-in Q&A sessions
  • 30-minute time slots
  • See other attendees
  • Group problem-solving
  • No pre-work required

Recording Library

  • All past sessions saved
  • Searchable by topic
  • Timestamped chapters
  • Transcript available
  • Key takeaways summarized
