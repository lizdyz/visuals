## 🔍 Framework Spotlight: The Strategic AI Enablement Lens

*A framework for understanding how AI enablement creates value—and designing your deployment strategy*

Every effective AI enablement strategy works across **four dimensions**. Miss one, and you're either underinvesting or misdirecting resources.

### **The Four Dimensions**

| **DEPTH** | **REACH** |
|-----------|-----------|
| How deep does transformation go? | How many people benefit? |
| • Awareness (surface understanding) | • Intimate (5-10 people, VIP) |
| • Capability (can build and use) | • Regional (12-25, high engagement) |
| • Mastery (sustained practice) | • National (100+, broad awareness) |

| **LEVELS** | **COMMITMENT** |
|------------|----------------|
| How many organizational layers benefit? | What timeline matches your strategy? |
| • Your team (strategy, sales, marketing) | • Test (proof of concept) |
| • Your advisors (transformation) | • Deploy (quarterly activation) |
| • Their clients (cascade value) | • Embed (annual partnership) |

### **The Lens in Action**

**Use it to learn:** "Why did that wholesaler relationship deepen so much?"
*Analysis: They activated all 3 levels—trained their team, enabled advisors, helped advisors serve clients*

**Use it to create:** "What's our AI enablement strategy?"
*Design: Be intentional about all four dimensions—choose depth, reach, levels, commitment*

Once you have this lens, you see these dimensions everywhere. In competitors' approaches. In what's working vs what's not. **The lens doesn't just organize—it reveals patterns you couldn't see before.**

**Q: Do credits expire?**
Yes. Quarterly for Starter/Quarterly packages, annually for Annual. Encourages activation.

**Q: Can we mix virtual and in-person?**
Absolutely. Deploy credits however creates most value through your lens.

**Q: How do we decide which pattern fits?**
Use the four-dimension lens: What depth? What reach? How many levels? What commitment timeline?

**Q: Can credits go toward custom development?**
Yes. Custom workshop builds are 5 credits (design + first delivery). Repeats are standard cost.

**Q: What if our strategy changes mid-year?**
That's the flexibility of credits. Respond to what your network needs.
