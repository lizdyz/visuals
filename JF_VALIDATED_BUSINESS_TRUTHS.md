**BRILLIANT TESTIMONIAL STRATEGY:**
Jean-François has developed a phenomenal referral process:
1. Uses notes and comments from keynote session (Grain AI transcripts)
2. Crafts a testimonial in the client's voice based on their actual words
3. Sends draft to client: "Does this capture your experience? Would you attest to it?"
4. Because he did the legwork, clients overwhelmingly agree (very little friction)
5. Then asks: "Would you be willing to share this on your LinkedIn network?"
6. Creates buzz and visibility in their network
7. **Results:** Has gotten referrals directly from this LinkedIn sharing strategy
