# Baseline Assessment Framework
## Measuring Your Before/After

**This is how we ensure outcomes are based on YOUR reality, not arbitrary benchmarks.**

---

## What We Capture Before Workshops

### **Advisor Engagement Baseline**

**Questions we ask:**
1. How many advisors typically attend your training webinars?
2. What % of invited advisors actually show up?
3. What % complete any post-training action items?
4. How would you describe current engagement: High / Medium / Low?

**Why this matters:** We measure YOUR improvement in engagement, not vs industry average.

---

### **LinkedIn Activity Baseline**

**For advisors participating in LinkedIn workshop, we capture:**
1. Current profile completeness (headline complete? about section filled?)
2. Current posting frequency (posts/month)
3. Current engagement level (likes, comments, connection requests)
4. Do they have any content strategy documented?

**Why this matters:** We measure activity increase from THEIR starting point.

**How we capture:** Pre-work form asks these questions. Takes 15 minutes.

---

### **Tech Stack Baseline**

**For advisors participating in Tech Stack workshop, we capture:**
1. Do they have a complete tool inventory documented? (Yes/No)
2. How many tools are they aware they're paying for?
3. Have they identified any redundancies? (Yes/No)
4. Do they know what features are available in their tools? (Rate 1-5)
5. When's the last time they optimized their tech stack? (Never / >2 years / <2 years / <6 months)

**Why this matters:** We measure what they discover relative to where they started.

**How we capture:** Pre-work form documents their current state.

---

### **Wholesaler Relationship Depth Baseline**

**We ask wholesalers:**
1. What % of conversations are product-only vs strategic?
2. How many advisors view you as innovation partner vs vendor?
3. How often do advisors reach out proactively (vs you initiating)?
4. Rate relationship depth: Transactional / Growing / Strategic

**Why this matters:** We measure relationship quality shift from YOUR baseline.

---

## What We Measure After Workshops

### **30-Day Check-In**

**LinkedIn Workshop:**
- How many advisors published updated profiles?
- How many are posting more frequently than before?
- Activity change: [X] advisors went from [Y] posts/month to [Z] posts/month

**Tech Stack Workshop:**
- How many advisors completed their tool inventory?
- How many requested feature activations from IT?
- Cost savings identified per advisor (from THEIR baseline)

**Engagement:**
- Implementation rate: What % actually deployed what they built?
- Feedback quality: What are advisors saying?

---

### **90-Day Follow-Up**

**Sustained Change:**
- Are advisors still using Custom GPTs?
- Has LinkedIn activity sustained or dropped back to baseline?
- Have tool optimizations been implemented?
- Are advisors requesting more workshops/cohorts?

**Network Effects:**
- Are non-participants asking about the workshops?
- Are advisors sharing wins with peers?
- Have champions emerged who influence others?

---

### **Quarterly Business Review (If included)**

**Your Metrics:**
- Engagement trends across all workshops deployed
- Regional differences (what resonates where)
- Advisor segment insights (who implements most successfully)
- ROI indicators (qualitative + quantitative)
- Credit deployment optimization (where to invest next quarter)

## Why This Approach Works

**Traditional Problem:**
Vendors claim "85% implementation rate!" but against what baseline? Whose data?

**Our Approach:**
We measure YOUR advisors against THEIR starting point.

**Example:**
- Baseline: 10% of advisors post on LinkedIn monthly
- After workshop: 35% post monthly
- **That's 3.5× improvement for YOUR network**

Not comparing you to anyone else. Comparing you to yourself.
